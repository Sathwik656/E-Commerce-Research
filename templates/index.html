Risk segments (mathematically defined)

Risk value is R = P(churn) × Ŝ, where P(churn) is the churn probability and Ŝ the predicted next-order spending (BRL). Thresholds are the 33rd / 66th percentiles of the held-out predicted-spending distribution (stored in models/risk_thresholds.json): t1 = {{ risk_thresholds.low_max }}, t2 = {{ risk_thresholds.medium_max }}.

Prototype estimate. Both models are trained on the same unified Olist customer population (repeat customers with ≥ 2 delivered orders). The churn target is heavily imbalanced (~96% of repeat customers are dormant within 150 days), and the spending target is conditional on the customer placing a next order. The Customer Risk Value (“Churn Probability × Predicted Next-Order Spending”) is a prototype, risk-weighted spending indicator — not a validated estimate of recoverable revenue, profit, or the causal financial impact of churn. Rows are matched by customer_id when present in both files, otherwise by row order. Provide your own data or the included sample_inputs/ files for demonstration.
